# Feder for Cursor and Grok Bot

Every number counted. Every signal in one place.

Feder checks every sale, booking and form fill right where it happens, so you always know which numbers to trust. This plugin lets your assistant read that, in plain words.

## What it can do

It's read-only. Your assistant can:

- **Summarize your tracking:** each business's live sites, the events Feder watches, open breaks and breaks fixed in the last 30 days.
- **Look at one check:** whether it's on, whether its last run failed and when it last succeeded.

It can't change anything in Feder, and it never sees your contacts, billing, people, connection tokens or ad-platform data.

## Connect

1. Install the plugin. Your assistant connects to `https://app.getfeder.com/api/mcp`.
2. Sign in to Feder when asked, and allow read access (`feder.read`) on Feder's consent screen.
3. Ask something like "Is my tracking healthy?" or "What broke this week?"

You'll need a Feder account. Reading through the plugin is free. Plans and upgrades are managed on getfeder.com, never through the plugin.

To disconnect, remove the plugin or revoke access in Feder under Settings, AI assistants.
